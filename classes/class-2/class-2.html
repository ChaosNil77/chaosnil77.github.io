Try, Try, Try Again

16th September 2026

Have you ever thought about giving up? Today, we thought about the opposite of giving up — perseverance! We talked about what can happen when we keep trying, even when something feels tricky. We then put this into action with some fun challenges, including balancing books on our heads and trying to walk and then hula hooping! Some of us could do it, and some of us couldn’t (yet!), but the best part was cheering on and supporting our friends. We discovered that trying, encouraging others and believing in ourselves can make us feel amazing! We are all amazing, and we can all achieve!

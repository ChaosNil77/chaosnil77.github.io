St Eanswythe's PTFA

St Eanswythe's PTFA (Parent Teacher and Friends Association) is a registered charity dedicated to supporting our school, enriching the lives of our children, and bringing our wider community together.

Our main objectives are to run exciting events that engage the whole school community, and to fundraise so we can provide those special 'added extras' for the children—such as new play equipment and vibrant playground markings.

Who We Are & What We Do

We are a passionate group of parents, teachers, and friends working together to make a difference. Our key goals include:

  • Raising Funds: Enabling the school to purchase additional equipment to continually improve the children’s learning and play environments.
  • Organising Social Events: Providing fun, memorable activities for the children outside of regular school hours, such as school discos, sports days, and summer fairs.
  • Building Community: Bringing together parents, teachers, and local residents to support St Eanswythe's and create a strong, friendly network.

Get Involved!
